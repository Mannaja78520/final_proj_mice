🤝 Which modules work together

Modules only link to modules in the same group. A group is your installation — put your modules in one and they find each other by themselves, even with no venue WiFi. Another company's modules standing in the same room cannot join yours, and yours cannot join theirs, because a module's own hotspot password is derived from its group name.
