Light strip — module

← hub · works over WiFi or the USB cable, whichever this page was opened with

Pick a colour

Choose a colour and the strip follows. Adjust the brightness to suit your space.

Try an effect

A little motion, or a steady glow. The module keeps it running.

Play a saved light show
Upload a light show on the module page under Shows & files, then press Refresh here. It can play without this PC. A sequence YAML can script the strip with rgb / effect / bright / wait steps and loop: true — see sd_card_example/moves/rgb_show.yaml. Timed light shows, no PC needed once it runs.
Let the lights follow music
when you press Start the browser asks what to share — pick the screen (or the music tab) and tick “share audio”; the strip then follows whatever the PC is playing. Works in Chrome/Edge on this PC.
