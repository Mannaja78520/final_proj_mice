Find your way around

Every page has the same head. ← Hub is always first and takes you back to Home; Show technical details is always last and brings back addresses, ports and error codes on every page at once. In between: the page's name, a line saying what it is doing, and its own buttons.

Home has one row of tabs: Home, Tools, Modules, Firmware, Network, Settings and Complaints. STOP, Report, Shortcuts and Help sit in the head, so they are there on every tab. The tools and the module list work with nobody signed in; there is one place to sign in, and a card that needs it points there instead of showing a second login box.

A module's own page (and the light strip page) has the same head, with what the board is doing in words. Reading it needs no login; anything that moves the robot asks you to sign in and says so.

In Studio, the name of your moves and the one Save are in the head. Start with the sliders, then add poses to the timeline. Your saved moves are in the Sequence tab under Open saved YAML…. On a phone, Views opens the Top / Front / 3D buttons so they do not cover the robot.

The app pages (Cameras, Voice, Reconize, Photobooth, All-Jao) ask the hub whether their program is running, so they say the right thing on a phone too.

Mice opens in the Reconize look. For a dark hall, choose Midnight in the hub’s Settings.
