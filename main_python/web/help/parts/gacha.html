🎁 Prize machines — gachapon

One or more prize machines, each one board that turns a holder of pockets until a ball falls. This page decides which machine gives the prize, who may have one, and keeps a record of every prize.

On the machine itself

Open the machine's own page (Home ▸ Modules). Give one prize turns until a ball falls. Turn 1 pocket and pockets are for testing: count how many pockets it takes from the loading place until the ball drops and type that into Pockets until it falls on the Setup tab. Set the event name, start or close the event, the kind of prize and how many balls are in it.

Several machines

Add each machine with its board name, the kind it holds and where it stands. Machines with the same kind stand in for each other: when the one asked for is empty, the next one gives the prize and the robot tells the person where to go. Next to says which to try first.

Rules for an event

Per kind: how many there are and how many times one person may get one. People: a person's own limit or a special guest, by the name the face app knows. Give one anyway is for the admin: it works past the limits and is still counted. The rules can come from a Google Sheet with two tabs, items and people:

event, item, total, per_person, noteevent, person, item, times, special, note

Voice

Voice ▸ Questions shows what people ask about, by topic and by hour, and downloads every question as a file. An answer can also give a prize: the prize rules say which answers give which kind.
