🤝 Which modules work together
      
    

    
Modules only link to modules in the same group. A group is
      your installation — put your modules in one and they find each other by
      themselves, even with no venue WiFi. Another company's modules standing in
      the same room cannot join yours, and yours cannot join theirs, because a
      module's own hotspot password is derived from its group name.

🔌 Modules plugged in by cable
      
        
        
      
    

    
…

    
each port shows its result the moment IT answers — real
      USB-UART ports take ~2 s; Bluetooth phantom ports are recognized by their
      device type and skipped without opening them. A probed module shows who it
      is + every RS485 module behind it. “auto-check” re-probes every 12 s while
      this page is visible — it costs almost nothing (the PC just waits on the
      port), but the port is briefly busy, so turn it off while connecting
      Nong Studio over USB.
