## 2026-11-27

**New**

- The PC assistant keeps itself running; usage limits fall back to Codex, Gemini 4 Argon and small-step Gemini — `tooling`
  - *Problem:* the remote-control session dropped and came back with unfinished work, and someone had to type the start command by hand each time; a usage limit left the work stuck.
  - *Method:* double-click "Start PC Assistant" (and "Stop PC Assistant"): a low-priority check every 3 minutes keeps one session running, restarts it if it lost its connection or stopped writing its heartbeat, never touches other Claude windows, backs off after 3 starts an hour, and removes itself when the PC shuts down, restarts or you log in again. A new session is told which tasks are still "Doing now". At a usage limit the existing AI watcher hands the work on in order: GPT Codex, Gemini 4 Argon (full), regular Gemini (small steps only), one AI at a time, back to Claude when it is free.
  - *Numbers:* how to use and turn it off: docs/pc_assistant.md. Not turned on by the change itself; you start it with the .bat.

**Improved**

- Any AI turns the PC assistant's keep-running check on when it starts a task; after a restart the first message means continue — `tooling`
  - *Problem:* the PC assistant's keep-running check had to be turned on by a double-click, and after a restart nobody told the AIs to continue.
  - *Method:* whichever AI starts a task (Claude, GPT Codex, Gemini) runs python tools/pc_assistant_ensure.py first: it installs the check once (idempotent, no questions) unless the user pressed Stop PC Assistant, which leaves a "turned off" flag. The check has no logon trigger and removes itself when the PC restarts or shuts down; after a restart the user's first message to any AI means continue, and a new session is told to read HANDOFF.md and handoff/tasks, resume and run the ensure step again. The rule is in HANDOFF.md, the shared memory, the Codex and Gemini task-start text and the SessionStart hint.
  - *Numbers:* tested with throwaway tasks and a sandbox copy of the launchers; nothing was registered on the real PC. See docs/pc_assistant.md.
- check_one_module_list prints where the page's time went when the Home sentence is missing — `qc`
  - *Problem:* check_one_module_list failed in busy full runs with "'4 modules' not found in ''" and nothing said why (green alone, and 40 of 40 with 10 copies at once).
  - *Method:* the page now marks each stage (loaded, rows ready, before and after each scan) with milliseconds since load, and a new assertion says whether the page reached the Home sentence at all and shows those stages, so the next crowd failure explains itself. The Home sentence itself is never empty in the page (it always says counting / looking / no modules / N modules), so an empty read means the page did not get that far.
  - *Numbers:* the check passes alone (16 s).

**Fixed**

- check_no_undefined_names judges a page after it has loaded, not after a fixed 7 seconds — `qc`
  - *Problem:* in a busy full run the check judged a page after a fixed 7 seconds, before it had loaded, and reloaded it, so its "judged on its first load" assertion failed (green alone).
  - *Method:* it now waits until every page has loaded (up to 25 s) and then 2 s for the probes; the assertions are unchanged and a failure now prints what each page said.
  - *Numbers:* green alone (17.5 s); 8 copies at once pass 5 of 8 (the others fail only on the page-load time of 64 pages at once).

## 2026-11-26

**New**

- The Voice helper frees GPU memory when idle and wakes on the next question — `voice`
  - *Problem:* the Voice helper kept about 5.4 GB of the 12 GB graphics card even when nobody had asked it anything for hours, which makes CAD and the desktop stutter.
  - *Method:* after idleUnloadMinutes (15, 0 = never) without a request its models are unloaded from GPU memory (the process keeps running); the next question loads them again with a "warming up" status and the wake-up cost is kept for /health. Never during a question or in presence/greeter mode. Optional yieldVramWhen (for example SLDWORKS.exe) unloads after one minute while such a program runs.
  - *Numbers:* pending a measurement on the real helper (expect about 5.4 GB freed): tools/voice_idle_measure.py.
- One combined request file for the friend apps, charts viewable anywhere — `docs`
- PRs with the auto-merge label now merge themselves once the quick checks pass — `tools`
  - *Problem:* the owner had to type a merge approval for every PR.
  - *Method:* label `auto-merge` on a ready PR; GitHub Actions squash-merges it when the light CI check `quick-qc` passes. Only owner-added labels on same-repo branches count.

**Improved**

- GPU-memory planner measured on the real PC: card sizes 2 to 12 GB and spill vs swap vs hybrid — `voice`
  - *Problem:* nobody knew how the planner behaves on a small card, or whether moving jobs to the CPU or taking turns on the GPU is better.
  - *Method:* ran the whole voice helper on this PC with a pretend card of 2, 4, 6, 8, 10 and 12 GB and the whole card, with the face app, camera and background jobs running, and compared three ways of using the CPU.
  - *Numbers:* hearing takes about 4 s with models on the CPU (2 to 6 GB) and about 1 s from 8 GB; the planner as shipped wins; at 6 GB it keeps the wrong hearing model on the CPU; the "hybrid" way makes hearing 3 times slower. See docs/perf_vram_2026-11-26.md.
- QC, builds and measurement scripts now run at low priority so the PC stays usable — `qc`
  - *Problem:* a full QC run left the PC laggy and hard to use, and other programs are open in real use.
  - *Method:* QC (runner, workers, Edge launched through PowerShell, subprocesses), the exe build wrapper and our measurement scripts run at below-normal CPU, low disk and low memory priority inside a job object with a CPU cap and a memory limit. A governor watches the whole machine every 2 seconds and starts no new check while CPU is above 75%, free RAM below 6 GB or the GPU is busy (one check per pool always runs, nothing is stopped or killed). Off: MICE_POLITE=0, --exact-timing or "polite": false (the tool says it runs at normal priority). Optional stronger yield in qc/data/qc_speed.json. The real hub, Voice and Faces are never touched.
  - *Numbers:* see docs/experiments/experiment-records.md (2026-11-26 polite mode).
- Friend request: the face backend's video scheduler re-reads a 6.4 MB file 8 times a second — `docs`
  - *Problem:* the friend's face backend kept one thread busy (about 29% of a core) even with no job queued.
  - *Method:* py-spy on the running backend found the video scheduler parsing a 6.4 MB state file about 8 times a second; the cause, numbers and a one-time fix are in the friend request list (item 18), measured by stopping and restarting the backend for 5 minutes.
  - *Numbers:* stopping it did not change the PC's lag probes, so it is not the cause of the slowness; see handoff/tasks/lag-diagnosis-1126.md.
- Coordination notes mention the auto-merge label — `tools`

**Fixed**

- The GPU planner now puts the model that saves the most time on the GPU, and waits for a busy model instead of moving to the CPU — `voice`
  - *Problem:* measured 2026-11-26 on the PC: at 6 GB the small hearing model loaded first and kept the GPU while the Thai one (saves 2.5 s per visitor) ran on the CPU; at 10 GB a busy local AI sent a newcomer to the CPU, so a bigger card had fewer models on the GPU than 8 GB.
  - *Method:* an equally important model is replaced only by one that saves more time than everything it pushes out; a busy model is asked again until its idle moment (8 s, config) before the newcomer falls back to the CPU; the Thai hearing size uses the measured saving (2877 ms CPU vs 422 ms GPU).
  - *Numbers:* a QC check now walks 2 to 16 GB in 256 MiB steps and fails if a bigger card ever saves less time. Re-measurement: docs/perf_vram_2026-11-26.md.
- Voice idle unload no longer interrupts a model that is still loading — `voice`
  - *Problem:* measured on the real PC, the idle unload could fire while the local model was still loading and then said "asleep" with nothing in memory.
  - *Method:* it now waits for loading to finish, counts idle time from then, does nothing when no model is loaded, and records how much GPU memory an unload freed.
  - *Numbers:* about 4 GB of 5.1 GB freed after 30 s idle (test setting); the first answer after the sleep took 11.8 s, the next 0.4 s. See docs/experiments/experiment-records.md.
- check_voice_bench waits for the helper to answer instead of 15 s — `qc`
  - *Problem:* check_voice_bench failed in a full run at low priority: the real Voice helper needed more than the fixed 15 seconds to start listening (green alone in 58 s).
  - *Method:* the check now waits up to 60 s for the helper to answer and stops at once if the process died.
  - *Numbers:* green alone; with the wait cut to 0.01 s the check fails as it should.
- QC no longer leaves headless browsers running after a crash, Ctrl+C or a timed-out check — `qc`
  - *Problem:* 119 headless Edge processes from QC runs stayed alive on the PC (up to 67 h old, about 4 GB of memory) after killed runs, crashes and timed-out checks.
  - *Method:* the runner joins a Windows job object that kills everything it started when it ends (even if killed hard); Ctrl+C and SIGTERM end the run cleanly; every run starts by killing browsers whose owner is gone or older than 6 h (never a live run's); a full run fails if it leaves a browser behind. Proven with real processes in check_no_orphans.
  - *Numbers:* a hard-killed runner now takes its child and a detached grandchild with it within 15 s (was: they lived on).
- check_freeze_stop: more headroom for the page's beats in a busy run — `qc`
  - *Problem:* check_freeze_stop failed in busy full runs (the hub stopped the show because a busy page missed two beats) and passed alone.
  - *Method:* the hub's beat timeout in that part of the check is 6 s instead of 2.5 s (beats come every 1 s) and the page waits past it, so staying alive still proves it really kept beating.
  - *Numbers:* green twice alone; with the timeout set below the beat interval the check fails, as it should.
