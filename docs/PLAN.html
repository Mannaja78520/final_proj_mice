Working in parallel — what can go together

One QC run is 9 to 14 minutes and the gate must stay FULL (three times on 2026-08-18 a change broke a check that looked unrelated). So the saving is not in running less: it is in putting SEVERAL finished tasks through ONE gate. A0-7 + A0-8 + A0-9 cost one gate instead of three.

That only works when the tasks do not touch the same file. These are the lanes. Take at most one task from each lane into a batch, and edits inside a lane stay strictly one at a time.
