# app-faces

Face recognition app.

## Rules and traps

- Greeting (rules.py, A5) is OFF in the shipped rules.json; the screen turns it on.
- Their app publishes one match twice and the "kiosk" copy (not a place) can
  come first. State._note hands the later real-door copy to the greeter even
  though the merge swallows it, or nobody is ever greeted.
- History starts at latest_seq, then follows after_seq without a time lookback.
  node_id records provenance; only a fresh real node can greet. Delayed rows
  count without claiming presence. Busy is decided by the hub (whenBusy).
- rules.json has ONE writer, the watcher (POST /rules). The page saves through
  hub /api/faces/rules (login on POST); keys starting with _ are its notes and
  must round-trip. Milestones fired live in faces_fired.json, never rules.json.
- A13: what the brief asks of Face_Regonize (/api/look, present, strangers in
  the feed, call_as) is spoken only in friend_api.py, names from
  config/partners.json `asks`. Nothing is used until THEIR /openapi.json lists
  it; a look answer without `"kept": false` stops that camera. Our cameras
  (config/faces.json look) are read through the hub's relay, never the board.
  Strangers become arrivals through State.sighting (per camera, by count), so
  a repeating feed is not one arrival per frame. Fakes only: qc/lib/fake_friend.py.
- Photo test (A42-13): `tools/face_photo_test.py` sends still photos down every
  road a webcam frame takes (their look, watcher then Voice, Voice /identify,
  the running hub), timed. `--fake` is QC (`check_face_photos`); on the PC
  `--photos _photo_test --fetch-unknown --hub`. Photos of people stay out of
  git (`_photo_test/` is ignored): the repo is public.
- A relay hands a viewer arriving when nobody watched only a NEW picture
  (cam_relay `since`): the old one went out as "now" (`check_pc_camera`).
