# app-faces

Face recognition app.

## Rules and traps

- Greeting (rules.py, A5) is OFF in the shipped rules.json; the screen turns it on.
- Their app publishes one match twice and the "kiosk" copy (not a place) can
  come first. State._note hands the later real-door copy to the greeter even
  though the merge swallows it, or nobody is ever greeted.
- A history row has no camera: counted, never greeted. Busy is decided by the
  hub (whenBusy on the request), never by reading the queue first.
- rules.json has ONE writer, the watcher (POST /rules). The page saves through
  hub /api/faces/rules (login on POST); keys starting with _ are its notes and
  must round-trip. Milestones fired live in faces_fired.json, never rules.json.
