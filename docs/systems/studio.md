# studio

Nong Studio: the pose editor, timeline and live 3D monitor.

## Rules and traps

- Read `nong/main_python_set_nong/CLAUDE.md` first: save a patch after every web
  change, and document every feature in its help page.
- Studio parses its own `dev` - it is opened with a concrete route, not `auto:`.
- `web/index.html` and `web/app.js` are BUILT (`python tools/build_web.py`): edit
  `web/html_parts/` + `index.template.html` and `web/app_parts/`. Read
  `docs/web_style.md` before changing the screen.
- A46-1 safe paths (`safe_path.js`, data `web/safe_path.json`): every move
  that copies a person (video, dummy follow, recording) or gestures while
  talking goes through planSafe/safeKeys: straight if clear, else RRT*
  + checked shortcuts; live follow holds the last safe pose when no way is found in
  liveBudgetMs. Video "stay with the music" passes keepTime (check_safe_path).
- Normal Studio motion uses checked_motion.js: fresh idle reported pose,
  checked rounded waypoints, one owner, arrival before the next leg. Timeline
  preview/Hub/native exports share checked legs; Stop cancels stale startup.
  Setup extraClearanceMm adds to the base4mm across body/head/arm/hand checks.
  The rig is a geometric proxy: dimensions/calibration and garment allowance
  must fit the real robot. Partial native runs play once to avoid an unchecked
  loop from a truncated prefix. Direct serial commands bind their port/bus.
- A45-28 palm hold (`hand_hold.js`, data `web/hand_side.json`): the side kept
  on top per hand is applied in the wrist drag (a small window per frame, the
  whole swing on release) and in gotoXyz; facePalm(..., {safe}) skips self-hits.
  The reach readout's target goes stale once that arm's joints change any other
  way (check_palm_hold).
- The talk-gesture fit (`talk_gestures.js`) moves `pose` while it works: it
  saves and restores it around every gesture, and runs on Sequence tab open or
  after TALK_IDLE_MS of no input, so it never fights a drag.

- A48-1 robot look (`urdf_view.js`, data `web/urdf_view.json`): the CAD look
  builds the URDF tree and points jointGroups/elbowBalls/wristBalls/palmMarks/
  bodyGroup INTO it, so IK, collisions, video, dummy, playback and monitor move
  the URDF chain (applyPose -> urdfApplyPose). Joint sense "auto" = sign of its
  axis vs the shapes' axis at zero. RIG.view keeps look/model/sizes/custom; the
  user's own sizes are never overwritten by the URDF's (check_urdf_look).
- A48-6 rig from the CAD (`rig_from_cad.js`, data `urdf_view.json` rigFromCad):
  RIG.after / RIG.mount / RIG.order (defaults change nothing) let the shapes
  equal the CAD; by default only sizes, shoulder spots and shrug pivot are
  taken (user: keep the rig's joints). Never zero/home/invert/limits/offset.
  Old rig in RIG.view.oldRig. rigChain() is the shapes' arm chain off-scene.
- A48-8: CAD look's drawn hand turned into the CAD hand's plane (urdfMeshPalm,
  data hands.*.palm "mesh", palm.from/flat); RIG.view.handMarks hides it.
- A48-9: fit.anchor "home" = zero at HOME is the CAD's drawn pose (a free zero
  twisted a shoulder block 179 deg). A48-10: viewFloor() grid show/height
  (data floor, RIG.view.grid); only the grid moves.
