When Voice answers a question that fits a row, the person asking gets that kind (the rules above still apply). Leave a box empty to not care about it. About is the question's topic (Voice ▸ Questions); Words are words in the question.

Games that give a prize

A win in the friend's games (All-Jao Games, opened from the hub) asks for a prize. The first row that fits gives its kind; the rules above still apply. Result: words in what the game shows, for example the wheel's prize name, the quiz score 5 or won for the memory game; empty = any win. Who: the person when the game does not name one (empty = whoever the camera sees).

Other PCs and forms

A program on another PC, a Google Form's script or a button in another app can ask for a prize with a key, without signing in here. One key per app; remove it to stop that app. A key can only ask: the limits per person and per event still hold, and it can never give one anyway.
