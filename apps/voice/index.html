Scenario

what the rig is doing
voice for this scenario

The words above tell the rig who it is being; the voice, speed and pitch are how it sounds while being it. A poem wants a slower speed than a shop greeting. A scenario voice wins over the voice settings, so one scenario can hold one voice for every visitor, whatever language they speak.

How it speaks Thai

polite word at the end of a sentence

The rig is one character, so it uses one polite word. It used to end one sentence ครับ and the next ค่ะ, which in Thai sounds like two different people. Any other one it writes is changed to this.

what it calls itself

The same reason: it used to say ผม in one answer and ฉัน in the next. Any other one it writes is changed to this.

room to finish a Thai sentence (words worth)

Thai costs two to four times more room than English, so the same setting that suited English stopped Thai answers in the middle of a word. Raise this if answers still end too soon; lower it if they take too long to arrive. An answer that is still unfinished is cut back to its last whole sentence rather than read out broken.

what it says when it has nothing to say

Said when the answer came out unfinished, or in the wrong language. Saying this is better than guessing - the rig never invents a password, a price, a time or a place.

Faces

forget a face after (seconds)
look again every (seconds)

After that many seconds with nobody in front of the camera, the rig stops using the name and answers the plain way. A look that sees nobody forgets the name at once, whatever this says.
