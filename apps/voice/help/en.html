🎤 Voice — talk and give commands by voice

A smart Q&A system and text-to-speech, switching smoothly between the computer's speaker and the speaker on the Nong robot.

Read the status at the top before you start speaking or listening. If it says the voice helper or the connection is not ready, check the connection and try again. The status on the web does not confirm that the robot's speaker really made a sound.

1 Switching speakers (PC vs Robot Speaker)
How to set it: Choose the target between PC Speaker or Nong Robot Speaker
What changes on the robot: • PC Speaker: Plays the sound through the main computer's speaker.
• Nong Speaker: Streams the sound over WiFi into the I2S chip (PCM5102A/TPA3118) on the robot, while the head tilts to the rhythm of the speech.
2 Speaking manner
How to set it: Choose a style: normal, polite, playful (Sassy), or recited.
What changes on the robot: It adjusts the sentence endings automatically (such as จ้ะ, นะคะ, ครับ) and tunes the pitch, high or low, and the speed of speech to show that mood at once.
3 Racing the voice speed (Fast-Race 650 ms)
How to set it: Turn on Fast-Race in the voice settings
What changes on the robot: The system calls the cloud TTS first. If there is no reply after 650 ms, it switches to the offline voice on the machine at once, so the robot never stands frozen.

Ask the rig a question

The Voice app in the Tools list: type a question, the rig answers. The answers come from a helper program that runs on this PC next to the hub (the hub itself never carries an AI — it only passes the question along and the answer back).

The helper starts by itselfnew

If the helper is not running, the hub starts it the moment anything needs a voice: opening the Voice app, a line the rig must say, or a talk gesture in Studio. The Voice page shows starting the voice helper… with the seconds counting; a question asked meanwhile waits and is then answered. If startup takes too long, the page says why. The hub keeps the same helper while it is still running, so another request cannot open a second copy. A late healthy reply lets the app continue; a helper that has stopped can be started again. If it cannot start, the page says why (its last lines are behind the technical switch, the full log is in .local/voice/helper.log). Which Python it uses and how long it may take are in config/voice_helper.json.

Speak instead of typingnew

The Voice page has a Press to talk button: press it, speak, press again to stop, and your words appear in the box ready to send. The listening runs on this PC too. Which languages it hears — and the words each language gets wrong often — live in config/voice.json; adding a language in config/voice.json (tts.voices / stt.languages) automatically adds its column and translation target in the Answers sheet and script detector. Use a plain code such as ko. The detector reads the alphabet, not the exact language: French, German, Spanish or Vietnamese questions land in the English column, and kanji with no kana lands in Chinese.

One voice at a timenew

If you press Try or ask a new question while an answer is still playing, the old answer stops first, so two voices never talk over each other. If the voice helper does not answer within a minute, the page says so and you can try again instead of waiting for ever. Leaving the page stops its voice.

The rig speaks one thing at a timenew

Everything the rig says goes through one queue on the hub: a greeting, an answer from this page spoken through a robot, a message from another program on this PC. So two of them never talk over each other. Something that arrives while the rig is talking either waits its turn or is skipped — whoever asked chooses — and a greeting that waited too long is dropped, because the person has walked on (maxAgeSeconds, 30 seconds unless changed).

How to use: The Answers tab — type the question people ask at the top, and the robot's answer below.

2 Questions dashboard and unanswered questions (Questions & Unanswered)
How to use: The Questions tab — see the analysis charts and the frequent questions.
What it can do: Shows statistics in 6 ways (topic, time of day, answer source, language, prizes, top questions) and a box that gathers questions people often ask but that have no fixed answer yet, with a button + Make an answer to add it to the answers sheet in a single click.

If Load the AI, Unload the AI or reading the status fails, the button comes back to press again, with an error message. Loading a real model can take a while depending on the machine.

The on-device AI (Ollama) starts by itself when used: You do not need to start it after switching on the computer, and it is not set to open at login. Pressing Load the AI or asking the first question opens it with a hidden window and waits until it is ready (normally no more than about 40 seconds). If it does not come up, it says why in plain words and does not retry for 1 minute; press Load the AI again.

Press the talk button and you see "opening the microphone…" at once. If the browser has not yet allowed the microphone, it tells you how to fix it, and you can still type the question instead.
